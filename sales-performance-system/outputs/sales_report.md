# Sales Performance & Revenue Optimization Report

**Data period:** 2014-01-03 to 2017-12-30  
**Records analysed:** 9,994 order lines across 5,009 orders

## 1. Overall Performance
- Total sales: 2,297,201
- Total profit: 286,397
- Overall profit margin: 12.5%
- Orders returned: 5.9% of all orders

## 2. Sales Growth
- 2014: sales 484,247, profit 49,544
- 2015: sales 470,533, profit 61,619
- 2016: sales 609,206, profit 81,795
- 2017: sales 733,215, profit 93,439
- Average yearly sales growth (2014 to 2017): 14.8%
- Sales growth in 2017: 20.4%

## 3. Product Performance
- Most profitable category by margin: Technology (17.4%)
- Weakest category by margin: Furniture (2.5%)
- Most profitable sub-category: Copiers (55,618 profit)
- Loss-making sub-categories:
  - Tables: -17,725
  - Bookcases: -3,473
  - Supplies: -1,189

## 4. Employee (Regional Manager) Productivity
The data links each regional manager to one region, so productivity is measured at manager level.

| Manager | Region | Sales | Profit | Margin | Avg Discount |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Anna Andreadi | West | 725,458 | 108,418 | 14.9% | 10.9% |
| Chuck Magee | East | 678,781 | 91,523 | 13.5% | 14.5% |
| Cassandra Brandow | South | 391,722 | 46,749 | 11.9% | 14.7% |
| Kelly Williams | Central | 501,240 | 39,706 | 7.9% | 24.0% |

Anna Andreadi (West) has the highest margin; Kelly Williams (Central) has the lowest.

## 5. Performance Gaps
- 18.7% of order lines lose money.
- Orders with no discount earn a 29.5% margin.
- Discounts above 20% make up only 13.9% of order lines but cause 89% of all losses.

## 6. Recommendations
1. **Cap discounts at 20%.** Heavy discounting is the main cause of losses. Require manager approval for any discount above 20%.
2. **Fix Kelly Williams's region (Central).** Its average discount of 24.0% is the highest of the four regions and its margin is the lowest. Review discount practice there first.
3. **Review loss-making sub-categories** (Tables, Bookcases, Supplies). Re-price them, cut discounts, or renegotiate supplier costs.
4. **Push high-margin products.** Focus marketing and sales effort on Copiers and the sub-categories with margins above 25%.
5. **Share best practice.** Use Anna Andreadi's approach in West as the model for other regions.
6. **Reduce returns.** About 5.9% of orders are returned; track the reasons to protect profit.

## 7. Limitations
- The dataset has only four managers, so employee productivity is measured by region.
- Analysis is based on historical data and does not prove cause and effect.
